1. Start and install

Open ges-promohub.web.app or GitHub Pages. Both run independently from the same application source. Sign-in and saved browser data are separate on each hosting origin. Install from your browser when supported, or continue in the browser. For offline study, keep the internet connected while the app and required books download, select Offline downloads in the library and download each required book, then test access offline.

The library contains GES Promotion Aptitude Test Pasco, Model Aptitude Test for GES 1 and 2, Effective Teaching Methods, Notes for Aptitude Test for GES, and Education in Ghana. Select a book to open it. This is a study resource, not a replacement for official GES notices or exam requirements.

2. Create an account and sign in

  1. Select Sign up, enter your profile name, email and password, then submit.
  2. Open the verification email. Registration returns you to login.
  3. Sign in and, if necessary, select Check verification in My Account.

Continue with Google uses your Google account. Start 7-day guest trial starts or resumes a guest device trial. A guest session has no cloud account. First login and account verification require internet. Use the same account on another device when you want paid cloud sync.

Forgot password on login sends a reset email for email/password accounts. Changing your profile name does not change your login email.

3. Read and move through a book

Use the bottom arrows or a horizontal swipe to move pages. Ordinary desktop clicks use left, centre and right reading zones. The centre toggles reader controls. Use the menu button for the sidebar. Contents lists chapters and nested headings; Bookmarks lists saved locations in the current book.

Choose a contents heading or search result to jump to it. Click book links and footnote references directly; these should not trigger page turning. External links can need internet and browser permission. Use Back to return to the library and choose another book.

Refresh restores the same book and its saved location in the current account or guest scope. After Back returns to the library, a refresh stays there. Missing or invalid saved sessions fall back to the library. The saved chapter and percentage can appear before the full book’s location calculation completes; first-time calculation can still take time. Pull-to-refresh is contained while login or profile is open.

4. Text, bookmarks and search

A− reduces text size and A+ increases it. Two-finger pinch handling inside the reader adjusts text size within limits. The font menu offers Serif, Open Sans and Arial. Images such as book covers do not resize as normal text.

Use the bookmark button to save your location. Open Bookmarks in the sidebar to return to a saved location or delete a bookmark. Lists are separate for each book. Use the search button, enter a term and choose a result in the current book.

5. Choose app and reading appearance

App appearance offers System, Light and Dark on login and My Account. The library appearance button cycles those choices. System follows the device’s light/dark preference. A manual choice is saved on that device. Login, library, account, administration and FAQ use the app appearance.

The reader palette offers Light, Sepia, Dark and Night for book content independently. Font and reading preferences are separate from app appearance. Embedded FAQ inherits the app theme and has no duplicate appearance dropdown; standalone FAQ retains its selector.

6. Trial, plans and renewal

The 7-day trial saves reading progress, bookmarks and preferences on this device only. It does not provide cloud reader storage or device syncing. Signed-in account and trial metadata still live on the server; guest trial state is device-local.

Paid plans enable cloud saving and device syncing for a verified active account in Online mode. Offline mode keeps reader changes on the device. The sidebar and My Account show access status, expiry and days remaining. Within seven days, My Account displays a renewal reminder. Select Renew subscription there or on an inbox reminder to open the existing plan and MoMo payment workflow. The button does not charge your wallet or automatically approve access. 7. Submit a manual MoMo payment

  1. Open My Account and choose your plan.
  2. Confirm the displayed amount and recipient. Pay that exact amount externally to MTN MoMo 0243443688, Abdul-Latif Ahmed.
  3. Copy the unique MoMo transaction ID from your payment SMS or receipt into the MoMo transaction ID field. Do not enter the payment description, such as PromoHub or your name.
  4. Select Submit payment for review. This submits a request; it does not charge your wallet.
  5. Use Check payment status. Wait for administrator verification and approval before expecting paid access.

Pending means awaiting review. Approved activates the verified plan. Rejected does not grant access. Keep your receipt. A previously credited transaction ID cannot be used to activate another account. Payment approval can take time; the app does not promise an automatic approval deadline.

8. Local data, cloud sync and offline use

Reading positions, bookmarks and preferences are scoped by account and book, with a separate guest scope. The 7-day trial saves reader data on the device only. Verified active paid accounts can sync in Online mode with an admitted reader session; administrators have cloud access without buying a plan.

To move between devices, finish saving on the first online reader, return it to the library, and then open the book on the second device using the same account. Offline-mode changes remain local until you reconnect and switch to Online mode. Verify the restored position before relying on it.

New sign-in, account verification, mode changes, payment submission/review, password/email changes and Administration require internet. Use Offline downloads in the library to download required books while connected. The manager shows file sizes, download progress and cached reader-file readiness. Download all books skips saved books; Cancel download stops the active download. Remove download deletes only the local book file, retaining progress and bookmarks. Downloads survive app-shell updates, but browser storage clearing or eviction can remove them. Files are shared by accounts in the same browser origin; they do not bypass subscription, mode or device requirements. Browser storage can be cleared or evicted; a cached book does not prove that reading progress reached the cloud. Keep important study notes separately.

9. Choose Online or Offline reading mode

Online mode is the default for subscribed accounts. It requires internet and an admitted reading session; disconnecting does not automatically switch you to Offline mode. With valid paid access, verification and active status, Online mode permits cloud saving and device syncing.

  1. While connected, open Profile → My Account → Devices and sessions.
  2. Choose Offline mode under Reading mode and select Save mode.
  3. Use Offline downloads in the library to save each required book. Open each downloaded book while still online to confirm the registered device. Test access offline before travelling.

Offline mode stays selected until you change it. There is no 24-hour timeout; ordinary paid access still ends at subscription expiry. Reading changes are saved locally and cloud syncing is paused, even when internet is available. To resume cloud syncing, reconnect, choose Online mode and select Save mode, then open the book and verify that syncing succeeds.

Changing either mode requires internet. A cached book alone does not grant offline access. Clearing storage removes device confirmation and cached files, so reconnect and prepare offline reading again. Trial and guest reading remain local-only; administrators are exempt from paid reader restrictions. Established legacy Premium accounts may not have a dated expiry.

An administrator can also change a user’s Reading access mode. Users can change their own mode online, so this is not an administrator lock. A disconnected device can retain an earlier offline permission until it reconnects or its subscription expires. Offline mode cannot guarantee one reader across disconnected devices.

10. Devices and simultaneous reading

Subscribed accounts allow 2 browser/PWA registrations and 1 online reading session. A registration identifies browser storage, not a physical phone. Different browsers, cleared storage and the Firebase/GitHub hosting URLs can count separately. Installing the PWA may share its browser storage, depending on the browser.

If another online reader is active, return that reader to the library with Back before opening a book elsewhere. After an abrupt disconnect, the online lease can take up to 2 minutes to expire. Close a duplicate reading tab in the same browser if asked. Opening Administration does not claim a reading session.

  1. Open My Account → Devices and sessions → Manage devices.
  2. Review registered entries and the active-session indicator.
  3. Remove an unused registration if the two-registration limit is reached. Removal requires recent authentication.
  4. Reconnect a device to confirm its current permissions before opening a book.

Removing a registration prevents its next server session confirmation. It cannot immediately erase a cached book or revoke permission on a disconnected device. Offline access is limited by saved permission and subscription expiry, and can allow more than one disconnected reader. Keep your login private; session limits do not make account sharing safe.

11. My Account and account security

Open the profile button to see your name, login email and access. Save name updates the profile name. Resend verification sends a verification email; Check verification refreshes its status. Log out ends the current session. Open Administration appears only for authorised administrators and opens a separate page in the same window. Select Back to PromoHub to return to your saved book and position. Tapping outside the profile closes it, as does its close button.

Expand Account Security. For Change password, enter current password, new password and matching confirmation, then submit online. Passwords must satisfy Firebase’s policy. For Change login email, enter current password and the new address, then select Send verification link. The old login remains until verification. Open the link and use Refresh verified email, or sign in again with the new verified address if requested.

Your UID remains unchanged, so subscription, reading data and administrator role remain associated with your account. Google-only users are directed to Google Account Security. Password fields are cleared after operations or closing, and credentials are not stored in Firestore or offline queues.

12. Administration, user search and payment review

As a verified active administrator, open Profile and select Open Administration. The dedicated Administration page opens in the same window with a read-only Overview. Select Back to PromoHub to return to your saved book and position on the same hosting URL.

Administration requires internet and checks your administrator role. An ordinary account cannot gain access by opening admin.html directly. On desktop, users appear in a three-column grid; medium screens use two columns and phones use one scrollable column. Expand Manage on a user card for that account’s controls.

13. Deletion, account recovery and Clear Data

Delete account requires typing DELETE and confirming recent authentication. It removes the cloud account and reader data. Local copies and retained audit/payment records may remain. This is irreversible. An administrator cannot self-delete while the admin claim remains; appoint another administrator before removing the existing one.

Deleting only a Firebase Authentication user directly is a different operation and does not run the app’s Firestore cleanup. Recreating the same email does not restore the old UID or automatically restore administrator claims. The project owner can appoint a replacement administrator using the owner process.

The library Clear Data button clears localStorage and origin Cache Storage, then reloads. It does not cancel a server subscription or delete the Firebase account. It can remove guest/trial-only progress, bookmarks, settings and cached books. First try ordinary reconnect, reload and reopen steps.

15. Troubleshoot and verify an update

  1. Check internet access and reconnect.
  2. Reload, close all PromoHub tabs/app windows, then reopen if an old version remains.
  3. For a paid account, save Offline mode while connected, open the required books, then test offline access. In Online mode, reconnect before reading.
  4. Confirm the same account, verification, active status and valid subscription when cloud sync is needed.
  5. For payment questions, check status and keep the original MoMo receipt.
  6. For credential errors, use current password or sign in again with the verified email. Use Google Account Security for Google-only credentials.
  7. Use Clear Data only after considering the loss of local-only reading data.

This guide is updated for app v1.9.5, documenting the reader and Administration features through v1.8.6. Notifications and administrator messages are available. The v1.9.2 release adds a stable offline book cache and download manager. The v1.9.4 release adds paid-subscription expiry reminders and days remaining. The v1.9.5 release adds the Administration overview and CSV exports. In v1.9.0, the bell appears immediately and Administration and the standalone guide open in the same window. Returning to PromoHub restores your saved book and position when access remains available. App version and service-worker cache version are independent numbers. Updates do not require creating a new account.

16. Notifications and administrator messages

Sign in with a verified account and select the bell in the library or reader. The inbox does not change your current book or reading position. Close it with Close, Escape or a tap outside the dialog. Badges count unread messages among the recent messages loaded: up to 100 personal notices and 50 announcements. Mark a message read or select Mark recent messages read while connected. Read status is saved to your account and can appear on your other hosting session when connected.

Personal messages are visible only to their recipient. Announcements are available to all active accounts, including trial and expired subscriptions, and accounts that join later. These communication records are separate from paid cloud reader storage. Previously loaded messages are saved on the device in the current account scope and can be viewed offline; reconnect to receive new messages or mark them read. Guest users do not have an inbox.

Automatic notices cover account creation, access changes, reading-mode changes and subscription payment submission, approval or rejection. Administrators receive new-account, explicit sign-in and pending-payment notices. Refreshing or restoring a signed-in session does not create a new sign-in notice. Delivery can take time; My Account and Administration remain the authority for current access and payment status. Paid subscribers receive inbox reminders at the 7-day, 3-day and 1-day stages before expiry. The server checks daily at 08:00 Ghana time and sends the most urgent current stage if an earlier check was missed. Each expiry/stage is sent once; renewal creates a new expiry cycle. Administrators, trials and undated legacy Premium access are excluded. Offline users receive new inbox notices after reconnecting. Closed-app push is not included.

In Administration, select Messages to send an announcement. For an individual message, open Users, locate an active account, expand Manage and select Send message. Enter a title and message, then confirm sending. Messages use plain text; recipients cannot reply through the app in this release. There is a 10-second wait between new sends. If sending fails, retry the unchanged message before changing it, so a retry can retain the same delivery ID.

Enable browser alerts is optional and requires browser permission. Alerts can appear for new messages when PromoHub is running in the background and the browser supports them. Old messages are not replayed as alerts at startup. Closed-app push is not included. Browser alerts may show message contents on the device screen; disable them if that is unsuitable. The inbox continues to work without permission. A notification tap opens the inbox for the matching signed-in account and keeps an existing reader on its current book.

17. Reading dashboard

The library now shows Continue reading, recent books, progress for each book and up to 12 bookmark shortcuts. Continue reading resumes the latest saved book and position. Bookmark shortcuts jump to the saved passage; the full list stays inside each book.

Each account and guest has separate local data. The dashboard works offline with saved device data; the book must also be downloaded and your subscription and reading mode must permit offline reading. Online cloud refresh is available only when cloud syncing is allowed. It checks the six books and up to 200 cloud bookmarks. Recent books are sorted by the latest opening on this device or saved progress. Progress can be unavailable until the reader finishes calculating locations.

18. Practice tests

Open Practice tests in the library reading dashboard. Choose a question set, topic and question count. Use a duration of 0 for untimed practice, or set a time in minutes. Questions and answer options are shuffled. Each correct answer earns one mark; wrong and unanswered questions earn zero, with no negative marking. Submission shows your score, explanations, sources and mistake revision.

The first set contains 12 adapted questions on assessment and learning domains. These are practice questions, not an official GES examination or an AI-generated live test. Closing a timed quiz does not stop its countdown. Resume saved attempt continues the same questions and deadline after refresh. Results and attempts are local to this account and browser origin, including during the trial; they do not sync to another device.

Save question set for offline saves it for offline practice. The built-in starter is precached with the app. Your trial/subscription, registered device and Online/Offline reading-mode checks still apply. Online mode requires an active online reader lease; standalone practice occupies the same paid reader slot. Offline mode needs existing device permission and unexpired access. Quiz answers and grades are device-local practice data and can be inspected or modified; they are not suitable for certified testing.

Administrators can open Administration → Practice tests, edit the starter or create/import a question bank, save drafts, review all answers and explanations, then publish. A bank supports 1 to 100 multiple-choice questions, each with a topic, 2 to 6 distinct options, one correct answer, an explanation and source. Published corrections appear on the next online download; an existing attempt retains its original question snapshot. Disabling stops signed-in online downloads; downloaded offline copies and the built-in guest starter are not remotely erased. Saving a draft preserves any earlier published edition.
