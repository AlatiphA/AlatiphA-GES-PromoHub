1. Start and install

Open ges-promohub.web.app or GitHub Pages. Both run independently from the same application source. Sign-in and saved browser data are separate on each hosting origin. Install from your browser when supported, or continue in the browser. For offline study, keep the internet connected while the app and required books download, select Offline downloads in the library and download each required book, then test access offline.

The library contains GES Promotion Aptitude Test Pasco, Model Aptitude Test for GES 1 and 2, Effective Teaching Methods, Notes for Aptitude Test for GES, and Education in Ghana. Select a book to open it. This is a study resource, not a replacement for official GES notices or exam requirements.

2. Create an account and sign in

  1. Select Sign up, enter your profile name, email and password, then submit.
  2. Open the verification email. Registration returns you to login.
  3. Sign in and, if necessary, select Check verification in My Account.

Continue with Google uses your Google account. Start 7-day guest trial starts or resumes a guest device trial. A guest session has no cloud account. First login and account verification require internet. Use the same account on another device when you want paid cloud sync.

Forgot password on login sends a reset email for email/password accounts. Changing your profile name does not change your login email.

3. Read and move through a book

Use the bottom arrows or a horizontal swipe to move pages. Ordinary desktop clicks use left, centre and right reading zones. The centre toggles reader controls. Use the menu button for the sidebar. Contents lists chapters and nested headings; Bookmarks lists saved locations in the current book.

Choose a contents heading or search result to jump to it. Click book links and footnote references directly; these should not trigger page turning. External links can need internet and browser permission. Use Back to return to the library and choose another book.

Refresh restores the same book and its saved location in the current account or guest scope. After Back returns to the library, a refresh stays there. Missing or invalid saved sessions fall back to the library. The saved chapter and percentage can appear before the full book’s location calculation completes; first-time calculation can still take time. Pull-to-refresh is contained while login or profile is open.

4. Text, bookmarks and search

A− reduces text size and A+ increases it. Two-finger pinch handling inside the reader adjusts text size within limits. The font menu offers Serif, Open Sans and Arial. Images such as book covers do not resize as normal text.

Use the bookmark button to save your location. Open Bookmarks in the sidebar to return to a saved location or delete a bookmark. Lists are separate for each book. Use the search button, enter a term and choose a result in the current book.

5. Choose app and reading appearance

App appearance offers System, Light and Dark on login and My Account. The library appearance button cycles those choices. System follows the device’s light/dark preference. A manual choice is saved on that device. Login, library, account, administration and FAQ use the app appearance.

The reader palette offers Light, Sepia, Dark and Night for book content independently. Font and reading preferences are separate from app appearance. Embedded FAQ inherits the app theme and has no duplicate appearance dropdown; standalone FAQ retains its selector.

6. Trial, plans and renewal

The 7-day trial saves reading progress, bookmarks and preferences on this device only. It does not provide cloud reader storage or device syncing. Signed-in account and trial metadata still live on the server; guest trial state is device-local.

7. Submit a manual MoMo payment

  1. Open My Account and choose your plan.
  2. Confirm the displayed amount and recipient. Pay that exact amount externally to MTN MoMo 0243443688, Abdul-Latif Ahmed.
  3. Copy the unique MoMo transaction ID from your payment SMS or receipt into the MoMo transaction ID field. Do not enter the payment description, such as PromoHub or your name.
  4. Select Submit payment for review. This submits a request; it does not charge your wallet.
  5. Use Check payment status. Wait for administrator verification and approval before expecting paid access.

Pending means awaiting review. Approved activates the verified plan. Rejected does not grant access. Keep your receipt. A previously credited transaction ID cannot be used to activate another account. Payment approval can take time; the app does not promise an automatic approval deadline.

8. Local data, cloud sync and offline use

Reading positions, bookmarks and preferences are scoped by account and book, with a separate guest scope. The 7-day trial saves reader data on the device only. Verified active paid accounts can sync while connected with an admitted reader session; administrators have cloud access without buying a plan.

To move between devices, finish saving on the first online reader, return it to the library, and then open the book on the second device using the same account. Offline changes remain local until you reconnect and the online session is accepted. Syncing then resumes automatically. Verify the restored position before relying on it.

New sign-in, account verification, payment submission/review, password/email changes and Administration require internet. Use Offline downloads in the library to download required books while connected. The manager shows file sizes, download progress and cached reader-file readiness. Download all books skips saved books; Cancel download stops the active download. Remove download deletes only the local book file, retaining progress and bookmarks. Downloads survive app-shell updates, but browser storage clearing or eviction can remove them. Files are shared by accounts in the same browser origin; they do not bypass subscription or device requirements. Browser storage can be cleared or evicted; a cached book does not prove that reading progress reached the cloud. Keep important study notes separately.

9. Automatic online and offline reading

PromoHub adapts automatically to your connection. There is no Online/Offline mode switch. A verified active paid account uses one online reading session and can sync reading positions, bookmarks and preferences while connected.

  1. While connected, sign in to the account you will use offline.
  2. Use Offline downloads in the library to save the required books.
  3. Open a downloaded book online once so this browser can save its device permission. Confirm the download and test access offline before travelling.

If the connection drops, a prepared browser continues reading downloaded books and saves progress locally. Saved paid permission lasts until subscription expiry; there is no added 24-hour cutoff. Reconnecting checks the account, device and online session before resuming cloud syncing. If another online reader holds the session, close it and retry. An explicit access denial does not fall back to saved permission.

A cached book alone does not grant access. A new browser or hosting address needs its own successful online check. Clearing browser data removes downloaded files and saved permission. Trial and guest reading remain local-only and stop at trial expiry. Administrators retain their verified device reading access. Established legacy Premium accounts retain their existing undated entitlement.

The two-registration limit remains. One online session is enforced by the server; disconnected devices cannot guarantee one reader across disconnected devices. Removing a device or suspending an account is checked on reconnection, and cannot immediately revoke a disconnected browser's previously saved permission.

10. Devices and simultaneous reading

Subscribed accounts allow 2 browser/PWA registrations and 1 online reading session. A registration identifies browser storage, not a physical phone. Different browsers, app installations and the Firebase/GitHub hosting URLs can count separately. Installing the PWA may share its browser storage, depending on the browser. Two entries labelled Mobile are not necessarily the same registration.

Live device updates

Sign into the same account on both devices. Open My Account → Reading and devices and keep the section expanded on the receiving device. The PC Reading access dialog also receives live updates while its registered-device list is open. While the app is online and visible, additions, removals and Reading now update automatically without closing and reopening the list.

Closed, background or offline apps cannot receive visible live updates. Return to the app and reconnect to refresh the list. If updates remain paused, select Manage devices in the profile or Registered devices in Reading access. A newly allowed browser appears after a successful reading-access registration; signing in alone does not necessarily add it. This browser marks the current browser registration. Reading now marks an unexpired online reader lease, not every signed-in device.

Remove or replace an unused registration

  1. Open My Account → Reading and devices → Manage devices.
  2. Select Remove device beside the unused entry. Keep This browser if you want to keep using it.
  3. If asked, confirm your current PromoHub account password, or confirm with Google if that is your sign-in method. The password is your PromoHub account password, not your Windows password. You stay signed in.
  4. After successful removal, the local list refreshes and other visible online lists update automatically. Removing from the profile does not open a book.

When opening a book at the registration limit, choose Registered devices in Reading access, then Remove this registration and open book beside an unused entry. Complete identity confirmation if prompted; the app retries the chosen book. No sign-out or data clearing is required. A new browser still needs its first successful online access check.

What removal and session limits mean

Removing one registration does not remove every device, delete the account, erase books or delete saved bookmarks and reading data. Removing This browser clears its saved permission and ends its current reading access. A removed browser may register again if admitted online and a slot is free. Existing server checks and heartbeats enforce removal on connected readers; changing a list is not an immediate sign-out of every device.

Before reading on another online device, use Back to return the first reader to the library. After an abrupt disconnect, its lease can take up to 2 minutes to expire. Close duplicate reading tabs if asked. Opening Administration does not claim a reading session. Administrator and local-only trial access remain exempt from ordinary paid-reader limits.

Removing a registration cannot immediately erase a cached book or revoke permission on a disconnected device. Offline access is limited by saved device permission and subscription expiry, and can allow more than one disconnected reader. Live list updates do not increase the registration limit, grant paid access or bypass account or session checks. Keep your login private.

11. My Account and account security

Open the profile button to see your name, login email and access. Save name updates the profile name. Resend verification sends a verification email; Check verification refreshes its status. Log out ends the current session. Open Administration appears only for authorised administrators and opens a separate page in the same window. Select Back to PromoHub to return to your saved book and position. Tapping outside the profile closes it, as does its close button.

Expand Account Security. For Change password, enter current password, new password and matching confirmation, then submit online. Passwords must satisfy Firebase’s policy. For Change login email, enter current password and the new address, then select Send verification link. The old login remains until verification. Open the link and use Refresh verified email, or sign in again with the new verified address if requested.

Your UID remains unchanged, so subscription, reading data and administrator role remain associated with your account. Google-only users are directed to Google Account Security. Password fields are cleared after operations or closing, and credentials are not stored in Firestore or offline queues.

The profile summary stays visible above the scrolling account settings: avatar, name, email, subscription status and Close remain in the header. Scroll the settings area to reach the controls below; there is no need to return to the top to close the profile. Settings are grouped into expandable Account, Subscription, Reading and devices, Appearance, Security and login, Notifications, and Data and account rows. Select a row to see its controls; select it again to collapse it. Renew subscription opens the Subscription row automatically.

Opening the profile no longer waits for the account-service refresh. A restored sign-in shows its name and avatar immediately. If sign-in is still restoring, the X remains available above a connection-status panel, with Open sign in as an explicit choice. Account and Appearance can be used while server details refresh; subscription, device, notification, security and data controls wait for the current account profile. Account details updating does not mean your plan has changed. Reconnect if details remain unavailable. Existing reading access, expiry and verification checks still apply.

On desktop, users appear in a three-column grid; medium screens use two columns and phones use one scrollable column. Expand Manage on a user card for that account’s controls.

13. Deletion, account recovery and Clear Data

Delete account requires typing DELETE and confirming recent authentication. It removes the cloud account and reader data. Local copies and retained audit/payment records may remain. This is irreversible. An administrator cannot self-delete while the admin claim remains; appoint another administrator before removing the existing one.

Deleting only a Firebase Authentication user directly is a different operation and does not run the app’s Firestore cleanup. Recreating the same email does not restore the old UID or automatically restore administrator claims. The project owner can appoint a replacement administrator using the owner process.

Open My Account → Data and account → Delete device data to remove PromoHub reading progress, bookmarks, practice results, settings and downloaded books saved in this browser. Unsynced changes will be lost. Your account, cloud data and server-managed subscription are not deleted. The action preserves the app shell, account/device access records and unrelated apps’ caches. Cloud data may return when syncing resumes. Delete account is a separate irreversible operation. First try reconnect, Retry and reopen steps.

15. Troubleshoot reading access, notifications and updates

This guide reflects app v1.11.8 and the reading-access and push-delivery recovery controls. An access warning or a missing alert does not, by itself, identify the cause or prove that a subscription expired.

Paid and online, but the book will not open

  1. Stay on the same hosting URL and browser, signed in to the intended account. Do not create another account or submit another payment just because a book is blocked.
  2. Allow the account restoration already in progress to finish. The reader waits up to 12 seconds before offering recovery. A temporary connecting label can appear before the subscription details load.
  3. In the Reading access dialog, read the message and select Retry opening book. An internet connection still has to reach Firebase; a Wi-Fi/mobile-data indicator alone cannot confirm that the access check succeeded.
  4. If two registrations are occupied, select Registered devices, review the labels and choose the unused registration. Confirm your current password or Google identity inline if requested. The app retries the selected book without requiring sign-out. A browser registration is not necessarily a separate physical device.
  5. If another Online reader is active, return it to the library with Back. After an abrupt disconnect, allow up to two minutes for its lease to expire. Close a duplicate reading tab when prompted.
  6. If the account is suspended, unverified or genuinely expired, follow that specific message. A valid saved Offline permission does not bypass a known server denial.

Offline reading and saved permission

A downloaded book and reading permission are different. Ready offline confirms the file is cached. Paid offline reading also requires this account’s valid saved device permission in the same browser and unexpired access. Download the required books and open one successfully while connected before relying on offline use. No mode switch is needed. A new browser or hosting URL cannot create its first device permission without a successful online check.

Reading switches automatically with connectivity. There is no added 24-hour cutoff; ordinary paid permission ends at subscription expiry. Valid saved permission covers a disconnection or temporary service failure. Cloud syncing pauses while access cannot be verified and resumes after a successful online session check. Saved on this device is expected while offline.

Message in the bell, but no system notification

  1. Check the new message inside the bell first. If it is missing there too, confirm the recipient account and the administrator’s send result before treating the issue as push-only.
  2. On the receiving device and URL, open My Account → Push notifications. Select Messages and announcements, Payment approval and/or Subscription reminders as needed. Select Enable / Save preferences and allow notification permission when asked.
  3. Keep the account signed in, press Home to put the app in the background, then have the administrator send one new direct message. Check the phone’s notification panel before reopening the app. Old inbox messages do not become fresh pushes merely because push was enabled later.
  4. If an alert arrives without sound or vibration, inspect that alert’s notification settings on the phone. A missing home-screen icon badge is separate from the bell’s unread count; its availability and appearance depend on browser, launcher and phone settings.
  5. To test message opening, tap the new notification. The updated route opens its specific message for the matching signed-in account, with All notifications to return. Older alerts without a message link can open the inbox. A missing or deleted message shows unavailable; do not switch accounts to try to bypass the account check.

Background push is optional and separate from paid cloud reading sync. A verified active account can receive messages while reading sync is paused. Permission is separate on the Firebase and GitHub URLs and in different browsers. The app cannot promise instant delivery, a sound, vibration or a particular home-screen badge.

Administrator: check the delivery outcome in PromoHub

  1. Open Administration → Push setup → Check push delivery. Find the recipient by name or email prefix, select the account and choose Check delivery.
  2. Check the master switch, account/verification status, browser registrations and alert categories. If a registration is disabled, ask its owner to select Enable / Save preferences on that device. Do not regenerate the public key as a routine recovery step.
  3. Send one new message through Messages, then return and check again. Compare the displayed last-attempt time with the test. The latest execution may be a retry of an earlier event.
  • Accepted by Firebase for delivery: Firebase accepted the send; phone receipt, display and sound remain unconfirmed.
  • Previously accepted, duplicate prevented: the same event/browser delivery was already accepted and was not sent again.
  • No enabled browser registrations: the recipient needs to enable push on the receiving browser.
  • No browser matched: inspect the category and listed skip reasons, such as an old registration or a browser registered after the notice.
  • Push delivery is disabled: check the administrator’s master configuration.
  • Account is not eligible: resolve account verification/status or deletion restrictions rather than changing the push key.
  • Notice is older than 24 hours: that push attempt is too old; the inbox message can still remain available.
  • Push delivery failed: record the sanitized error code and attempt time for Support. Do not assume every failure means an invalid browser token.
  • No check recorded: the checker has no recorded attempt since its update. Send a new message and check again; this status alone does not establish the cause.

The checker is read-only. It does not send a message, change permissions or prove that a device displayed an alert. It shows registered browsers without claiming to identify a physical phone from its push token.

Update safely and contact Support

  1. Reconnect and reopen the same URL. If an old profile redirect or older control remains, close all PromoHub tabs and app windows, then reopen so the waiting update can activate.
  2. For the GitHub URL, the project owner must wait for the GitHub Pages deployment as well as any Firebase deployment. Updating Firebase alone does not publish the GitHub frontend.
  3. Try ordinary reconnect, Retry and reopen steps before deleting device data. Do not uninstall or erase browser storage as a routine fix.
  4. If it still fails, contact Support with the exact warning, approximate time, browser/device, hosting URL, account email and what action failed. For push, include the delivery checker result. For a payment issue, keep the original receipt. Never send your password, verification codes, private push key or raw registration token.

16. Notifications and administrator messages

Sign in with a verified account and select the bell in the library or reader. The inbox does not change your current book or reading position. Close it with Close, Escape or a tap outside the dialog. Badges count unread messages among the recent messages loaded: up to 100 personal notices and 50 announcements. Mark a message read or select Mark recent messages read while connected. Read status is saved to your account and can appear on your other hosting session when connected.

Personal messages are visible only to their recipient. Announcements are available to all active accounts, including trial and expired subscriptions, and accounts that join later. These communication records are separate from paid cloud reader storage. Previously loaded messages are saved on the device in the current account scope and can be viewed offline; reconnect to receive new messages or mark them read. Guest users do not have an inbox.

Automatic notices cover account creation, access changes, reading-mode changes and subscription payment submission, approval or rejection. Administrators receive new-account, explicit sign-in and pending-payment notices. Refreshing or restoring a signed-in session does not create a new sign-in notice. Delivery can take time; My Account and Administration remain the authority for current access and payment status. Paid subscribers receive inbox reminders at the 7-day, 3-day and 1-day stages before expiry. The server checks daily at 08:00 Ghana time and sends the most urgent current stage if an earlier check was missed. Each expiry/stage is sent once; renewal creates a new expiry cycle. Administrators, trials and undated legacy Premium access are excluded. Offline users receive new inbox notices after reconnecting. Optional background push can be enabled in My Account → Push notifications.

In Administration, select Messages to send an announcement. For an individual message, open Users, locate an active account, expand Manage and select Send message. Enter a title and message, then confirm sending. Messages use plain text; recipients cannot reply through the app in this release. There is a 10-second wait between new sends. If sending fails, retry the unchanged message before changing it, so a retry can retain the same delivery ID.

Enable browser alerts is optional and requires browser permission. Alerts can appear for new messages when PromoHub is running in the background and the browser supports them. Old messages are not replayed as alerts at startup. Optional background push can be enabled in My Account → Push notifications. Browser alerts may show message contents on the device screen; disable them if that is unsuitable. The inbox continues to work without permission. A new notification tap opens its specific message for the matching signed-in account and keeps the existing book and position. Older alerts without a message link open the inbox. Select All notifications to return to the list.

17. Reading dashboard

The library now shows Continue reading, recent books, progress for each book and up to 12 bookmark shortcuts. Continue reading resumes the latest saved book and position. Recent books and Bookmark shortcuts start collapsed. Select either heading to show or hide its list; background updates keep your current choice until the page is reloaded or the account changes. Bookmark shortcuts jump to the saved passage; the full list stays inside each book.

Each account and guest has separate local data. The dashboard works offline with saved device data; the book must also be downloaded and your account must have valid saved reading permission. Online cloud refresh is available only when cloud syncing is allowed. It checks the six books and up to 200 cloud bookmarks. Recent books are sorted by the latest opening on this device or saved progress. Progress can be unavailable until the reader finishes calculating locations.

18. Practice tests

Open Practice tests in the library reading dashboard. Choose a question set, topic and question count. Use a duration of 0 for untimed practice, or set a time in minutes. Questions and answer options are shuffled. Each correct answer earns one mark; wrong and unanswered questions earn zero, with no negative marking. Submission shows your score, explanations, sources and mistake revision.

The first set contains 12 adapted questions on assessment and learning domains. These are practice questions, not an official GES examination or an AI-generated live test. Closing a timed quiz does not stop its countdown. Resume saved attempt continues the same questions and deadline after refresh. Results and attempts are local to this account and browser origin, including during the trial; they do not sync to another device.

Save question set for offline saves it for offline practice. The built-in starter is precached with the app. Your trial/subscription and registered-device checks still apply. Reading connectivity is automatic. Standalone practice occupies the same paid online reader slot. Offline practice needs existing device permission and unexpired access. Quiz answers and grades are device-local practice data and can be inspected or modified; they are not suitable for certified testing.

Administrators can open Administration → Practice tests, edit the starter or create/import a question bank, save drafts, review all answers and explanations, then publish. A bank supports 1 to 100 multiple-choice questions, each with a topic, 2 to 6 distinct options, one correct answer, an explanation and source. Published corrections appear on the next online download; an existing attempt retains its original question snapshot. Disabling stops signed-in online downloads; downloaded offline copies and the built-in guest starter are not remotely erased. Saving a draft preserves any earlier published edition.

19. Report a question error

After submitting a practice test, expand a question in Answer review and select Report a question error. Choose incorrect answer, unclear explanation, question wording or other, and describe the problem. Sending requires an active verified account and internet access. Guest results and scores remain local and unchanged.

The report includes the question edition, question text, displayed correct answer and explanation you saw. The administrator can compare that snapshot with the current published question. One report per account, question and edition is recorded; retries do not create duplicates. New reports are limited to one every 30 seconds and ten per day.

In Administration → Question reports, choose a status, inspect the reader report and snapshots, add a review note and mark Reviewed or Dismissed. Review does not edit questions or rescore saved attempts. When an administrator saves Reviewed, the reporting user receives one private bell notification: “Your question report has been reviewed. Thank you for helping improve PromoHub.” It does not mean a correction has been published. Optional push follows the user’s Messages and announcements preference. Dismissed reports do not send this acknowledgement, and administrator review notes remain private. Use Administration → Practice tests to correct a bank, check its answers and publish a new edition.

20. Optional push notifications

Open My Account → Push notifications. Choose message/announcement, payment approval and subscription reminder categories, then select Enable / Save preferences. Your browser must support push and you must grant notification permission. Setup requires internet access and administrator configuration. Each browser and hosting origin has its own choice; guest access cannot register push.

Alerts show generic text. Open the signed-in inbox for message details. Push is separate from paid cloud reading sync, so a verified active trial account can choose messages. Delivery can be delayed by connectivity, browser or operating-system restrictions. It is not a guaranteed emergency channel. The inbox remains available if push is off or unsupported.

Disable on this browser stops its local binding and unregisters it online. If disconnected, the local binding is cleared; browser settings can also revoke site permission. Account switching and sign-out clear the binding so queued alerts for another account are ignored. Previously opted-in accounts may restore their registration after signing in again without another permission prompt.

Administrators configure Administration → Push setup using the public Web Push certificate key generated in Firebase Console → Project settings → Cloud Messaging → Web Push certificates. Keep the private key private. The master switch pauses delivery. Only future announcements, private messages, approved-payment notices and subscription reminders trigger push. Old inbox messages are not replayed. Up to five push browser registrations per account are supported, separate from the reading-device limit.

Administrators can use Administration → Push setup → Check push delivery to inspect a recipient’s browser registrations and the last recorded attempt. A result of Accepted by Firebase confirms acceptance for delivery, not that a device displayed an alert. The check does not send a message or change permissions. After sending a new message, check again to see an acceptance, failure code or skip reason. Tapping a new notification opens its specific message; All notifications returns to the inbox.

21. Revision dashboard

Open Revision dashboard beside Practice tests in the library, from practice setup, or from a completed result. It uses up to 20 completed attempts saved for the current account or guest on this browser and hosting address. It works from device data offline. Results do not sync between devices and are removed by Delete device data.

Choose a question set, or All question sets. Practice accuracy is the total correct answers divided by the total questions, including unanswered ones. Topic cards show correct/total, accuracy and unanswered questions. Topics with fewer than three recorded question results say More practice needed. Below 70% accuracy with at least three results is marked Weak topic. These labels are study indicators, not official grades or predictions.

Improvement compares equal groups of up to five recent attempts with the preceding group. At least two completed attempts are required. The comparison uses question-weighted accuracy, not the average of test percentages. Different question sets, difficulty, repeated questions and mistake revision affect the comparison. Expand Recent attempt scores to see the latest ten results in your selection.

New completed tests save topic totals automatically without storing extra question copies in history.
