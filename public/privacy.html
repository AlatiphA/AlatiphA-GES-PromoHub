Privacy Notice

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This notice explains how AlatiphA Multimedia handles information when you use PromoHub to read books, practise questions, manage a subscription or receive administrator messages.

1. Information used by the service

  • Account details: your name, email, Firebase account identifier, verification status, sign-in provider and any profile photo supplied through Google sign-in. Email/password authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication. Your password is not stored in PromoHub’s profile documents or shown to administrators.
  • Reading information: saved book positions, percentages, bookmarks and reading preferences. Where paid cloud saving is allowed, these records can be associated with your account in Firestore.
  • Access and payments: trial/subscription dates, selected plan, submitted MoMo transaction ID, amount, payment review outcome, registered browser/device identifiers, device labels, reading mode and session timestamps.
  • Communications and security: personal administrator messages, announcements, notification read status, sign-in events and audit records of account or payment actions. Support correspondence includes information you choose to send.
  • Technical information: hosting and authentication providers can process connection information, such as IP addresses and browser information, for delivery, security and abuse prevention.

2. Why information is used

Information is used to authenticate accounts, supply reading access, save and synchronise permitted reading data, verify manual payments, prevent duplicate payment credit, enforce device/session settings, deliver service messages and expiry reminders, investigate problems and respond to support requests. We do not use your reading records to sell advertising.

3. Guest trials, offline files and browser storage

Guest reading data and trial reading progress stay on the browser/device. Registering an account still creates cloud authentication and profile records even when cloud saving of trial reading data is unavailable. Practice-test attempts, scores and downloaded question sets are local to the current account or guest on that browser; quiz results do not currently synchronise to the cloud.

LocalStorage, session storage and browser caches retain settings, trial dates, account-scoped reading information, session permissions, downloaded EPUB files and saved messages. Downloaded book files in a browser may be reused by different local accounts, while reading records are scoped separately. Firebase Hosting and GitHub Pages have separate local browser stores. Clearing site data, uninstalling some browser environments or storage eviction may remove local-only information and downloads. Logging out does not necessarily erase every saved file or local record.

4. Providers and administrator access

PromoHub uses Google Firebase Authentication, Firestore, Cloud Functions and Firebase Hosting, and is also published through GitHub Pages. External libraries may be loaded from content-delivery networks. These services process the information necessary to provide their features and have their own privacy practices. See Firebase privacy information, Google’s Privacy Policy and GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Authorised administrators can review account identities, access settings, payment records and operational audit information, and can export administration reports. This does not make those records public to other readers. Submitted payment IDs are used to match payments and prevent reuse; no MoMo PIN is requested. Your information is not sold by PromoHub.

5. Location and security

The current Firestore database is configured in South Africa, and callable Functions use a United States region. Hosting, authentication and provider infrastructure may process information in other countries. Regional settings do not mean every service record remains in one country.

Account access checks, server-managed privileges, security rules and secure network connections help protect service data. Offline data on a shared or unlocked device can be accessible to people using that browser. No internet service or device storage can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect your device and account credentials.

6. Retention and account deletion

Account and cloud reader records remain while the account is in use unless removed through available controls or an applicable support request. Account deletion removes the Firebase account and its profile subcollections through the app’s deletion process. Payment ledgers, audit records and a deletion marker can remain outside the deleted profile to support payment verification, fraud prevention and deletion safeguards. Account deletion does not automatically refund a payment or erase local copies on all devices. Contact support to ask about retained records; requests are considered in light of applicable legal obligations.

7. Your choices and rights

You can edit your profile name, manage credentials, switch permitted reading mode, remove downloaded files and request account deletion through My Account. Optional browser alerts require browser permission; you can disable them in the app or browser settings. Disabling alerts does not disable the in-app inbox. No behavioural advertising or analytics feature is enabled in this release.

Subject to Ghana’s Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), you may request access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, and deletion or objection where applicable. Contact us using the details below; we may verify your identity before acting. You may also raise a privacy complaint with Ghana’s Data Protection Commission. This notice does not remove your statutory rights.

8. Children, outside links and changes

PromoHub is intended principally for teachers and education professionals. Please do not submit pupil records or unnecessary information about children. External book/source links and payment providers operate under their own policies. Material changes to this notice will be reflected in an updated notice and, where appropriate, an in-app announcement.
